# DECISIONS AMENDMENT — The Re-Founding
### July 16, 2026 · Supplements the governing decisions document. Where this conflicts with prior decisions, this wins.
*Trigger: the founding document session. Everything below was decided in that session and is now locked unless Tim unlocks it.*

---

## 1. The founding document governs

A founding document now exists (current version: v4). It sits above the decisions document in the hierarchy:

- **The founding document says why anything exists. The decisions document says what we've settled. When the two conflict, the founding document wins.**
- The founding document is a public document — written to the spiritually homeless reader, not to the architects. It is likely the first thing people read, possibly the front page of the rebuilt site. It is never buried in a foundations folder.
- Its title is undecided ("The Founding Document" is a placeholder). Title waits on the project naming question — the two should rhyme.
- Every future document answers the emotional question before the theological one. This is now a standing rule.

## 2. The audience is named: spiritually homeless

- The person this is built for is **spiritually homeless** — still wanting Jesus, unable to do what church became. Includes people who left, people who never entered, and people still in a pew. It is a human condition, not a demographic.
- "Spiritually homeless" replaces "deconstructed," "exvangelical," "seeker," "unchurched," and all audience-segment language in every document.
- **Home** joins the table as governing metaphor. Home, table, belonging — the emotional architecture. We don't recruit; we welcome. We don't create members; people come home.

## 3. The two emotional tests (design requirements, not slogans)

Every page, practice, and decision is tested against two sentences:

1. **"Finally."** (relief)
2. **"I thought I was the only one."** (belonging)

If something makes fewer people say them — however smart, impressive, or theologically precise — it goes. Corollary promise: **no one should ever leave a table feeling more alone than when they came.**

## 4. Language kills (full-sweep, all documents, all pages)

- **"Alternative to traditional Christianity" / "alternative to Christianity"** — killed everywhere, including Tim's own working vocabulary. It reads as replacement and makes pastors adversaries. Replacement framing: *a home for people who still want Jesus.*
- **"Jesus is protected, not worshiped"** — retired. The durable sentence is: **"We protect people's access to Jesus."**
- **"Not the Bible"** as a standalone list item — retired. The conviction survives at full strength, positioned as a conclusion, in locked wording: *"Scripture gets brought back to Jesus, not the other way around."*
- The movement never defines itself primarily against Christianity or against churches. Invitation language, not opposition language.

## 5. The firewall (replaces the in-project coaching decision)

**Superseded:** the decision placing coaching tiers (Launch Coaching, Year One Alongside, Rebirth/Plant engagements) inside the project.

**New decision:**
- All paid professional work — coaching, consulting, speaking, books — lives under **Timothy Eldred's name, outside the project.** It is his profession. The project is his calling. The two never funnel into each other.
- The project site **may link out** to his professional work. It **never sells from within.** No pricing pages, no tiers, no engagement descriptions inside the project.
- The project is never used as lead generation. Nobody at a table is ever sold anything.
- The scholarship commitment survives, restated on the professional side: paid church engagements fund equivalent help for churches that can't pay.
- Money inside the project: donations only, non-tax-deductible as previously decided, buying nothing — no influence, no naming rights.

## 6. Founder-light (replaces "faceless")

- The goal is not facelessness — it's **founder-light.** Tim's voice matters in the early years; his name must not. Architecture assumes from day one that he's gone.
- The founder's story may appear where honesty requires it; the founder's identity never becomes load-bearing.

## 7. Theology placement: the library, not the lobby

- **The Whole Gospel informs the project; it is never the price of admission.** TWG is the library, not the lobby. Nobody must read or accept it to belong at a table.
- TWG and the table community must never collapse into each other. The community is never "The Whole Gospel Movement" or built around any teaching requiring agreement.
- Interior-awakening / "true second coming" language: **library only.** It is never stated as the purpose of the project, because a stated theological destination contradicts belonging-before-agreement.

## 8. Architecture: questions, not audiences (supersedes two-doors)

**Superseded:** the two-doors architecture (church door / no-church door) as the site's organizing fork. Splitting by institutional affiliation builds around audiences; we build around the questions people arrive carrying.

**New architecture — four spaces:**
- **The Table** — experience, belonging, practice (starter material, Canon of Fifty)
- **The Library** — TWG, theology, deep dives
- **The Workshop** — how-to: hosting, leadership, church adoption (the postures live here)
- **The Commons** — stories, shared life, evidence that other tables exist. *Does not yet exist; it is the missing room, and "I thought I was the only one" cannot be delivered without it.*

The site's emotional journey: **alone → found my people → found Jesus again → a new way to live → help others find home.**

Church-facing material survives — churches remain fully welcome users of the model, with the three postures intact — but "church vs. no church" is a Workshop distinction, not the front door.

## 9. The one-question rule (governs all future documents)

**A document may serve multiple readers when they share the same question. It splits when they carry different questions.** One question, one document. The corollary: we likely need fewer documents, not more — near-duplicates created by the old audience split get merged in the journey-map pass.

## 10. Site: rebuild, not recomposition

**Superseded:** the recomposition pass. The site gets **composed new** from the founding document and the journey map. Nothing proceeds on the site until the journey map exists on paper.
